Setup
How it works
A simple setup that runs on its own after configuration.
01
Connect your Social Media account
Authorize AInform Daily Post via official OAuth flow. Your credentials are stored only in your local n8n instance.
02
Configure your workflow
Set your posting schedule, content source, and any preferences in the n8n workflow editor.
03
Add your content
Point the workflow to your video files and captions. Supports any content type or niche.
04
Let it run
The workflow publishes automatically on your schedule via Content Posting API. No manual action required.
