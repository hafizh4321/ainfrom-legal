01 Overview

AInform Daily Post is a private, single-user automation tool. It is not a public-facing app or service. No user accounts are created, no sign-up is required, and no personal data is collected from third parties or visitors.

This service does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data. Period.

02 Information We Do Not Collect

AInform Daily Post does not collect any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • IP addresses or device identifiers
  • Browsing behavior or usage analytics
  • Location data
  • Payment or financial information
  • Any personally identifiable information (PII)

03 Information Processed in Transit

When the service operates, it processes the following data in real-time solely to perform its automation functions:

Data Type Purpose Retained?
Content (text, images, captions) Publishing to connected platforms No
API responses from platforms Confirming post success/failure No
Workflow execution logs Debugging within n8n (local only) Local only

No data is transmitted to any AInform Daily Post servers. All processing occurs locally within the user's own n8n instance.

04 API Credentials & Tokens

OAuth tokens and API credentials used to authenticate with third-party platforms and online services are:

  • Stored locally within the user's own n8n environment
  • Never transmitted to or stored by AInform Daily Post
  • Under the sole control of the account owner
  • Revocable at any time through each platform's developer or account settings

05 Third-Party Platforms

This service may send content to third-party social media platforms and online services on behalf of the account owner via their official APIs. Once content is submitted to a platform's API, that platform's own privacy policy governs how it is handled.

AInform Daily Post has no control over and takes no responsibility for the privacy practices of any third-party platform or service connected through this tool. The account owner is encouraged to review the privacy policy of each platform they choose to connect to.

06 Cookies & Tracking

AInform Daily Post does not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics scripts, or any third-party tracking technology. No behavioral data is collected from visitors to pages associated with this service.

07 Children's Privacy

This service is not directed at, designed for, or intended to be used by individuals under the age of 18. AInform Daily Post does not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a minor has used this service, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action.

08 Data Security

Because AInform Daily Post does not collect or store personal data on external servers, the primary security responsibility lies with the user's local n8n environment. We recommend:

  • Keeping your n8n instance and server software up to date
  • Using strong, unique credentials for your n8n instance
  • Restricting network access to your n8n deployment
  • Rotating API tokens periodically and revoking unused ones

09 Data Retention

AInform Daily Post retains no personal data. Workflow execution logs stored within your local n8n instance are entirely under your control and can be deleted at any time through the n8n interface. Content published to third-party platforms is subject to those platforms' own retention and deletion policies.

10 Your Rights

As no personal data is collected by AInform Daily Post, there is no data held about you to access, correct, or delete. If you have concerns about data held by a connected platform, please exercise your rights directly through that platform's privacy settings or data request process.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
